# Community Moderation Policy — Review Web

## 1. Core Mission & Philosophy
Review Web is dedicated to serving the citizens and visitors of Tirunelveli District, Tamil Nadu, with authentic, civil, and reliable community feedback.

Reviews reflect personal experiences. Reviews must not present unverified allegations as established factual judgments.

---

## 2. Prohibited Content
Reviews will be moderated and removed if they contain:
1. **Defamatory, False, or Fraudulent Content:** Fabricated stories, competitor attacks, paid promotional content, or libelous accusations.
2. **Hate Speech & Obscenity:** Vulgarities, religious slurs, caste-based abuse, or violent threats.
3. **Invasion of Personal Privacy:** Publishing phone numbers, personal home addresses, Aadhaar numbers, or medical records of individuals, doctors, staff, teachers, or students.
4. **Minor Protection:** Disclosing private personal details of schoolchildren or minors. For schools and colleges, reviews must focus strictly on institutional infrastructure, academic atmosphere, and campus amenities.
5. **Conflict of Interest:** Business owners or employees reviewing their own establishment under disguised identities.

---

## 3. Distinction from Official Grievance Filing
- A review on Review Web is an individual community reflection.
- Review Web is not a government court, police station, consumer forum, or municipal ombudsman.
- Where an establishment has a verified, active official complaint portal or helpline, Review Web provides direct access via the **Official Support / Complaint** modal.
- Submitting a review does **not** file an official legal grievance.

---

## 4. Moderation Workflow
1. **Community Flagging:** Any signed-in citizen may report an abusive review with reason and context.
2. **Review Queue:** Flagged reviews are queued in the Administrative Portal (`/admin-portal`).
3. **Decisions:**
   - **Dismiss Report:** Review remains published if it represents a fair subjective experience without policy violation.
   - **Uphold & Hide Review:** Offending review is hidden from public display; the establishment's average rating and distribution are recalculated automatically.
4. **Audit Trail:** Every moderation decision is permanently recorded in `moderation_auditlog` with administrator ID, timestamp, and decision rationale.
